How it works

No directory. No paid placements. Just the map.

01

We find you

With your permission, BrewSpot centers the map on your location — or you can search any city, area, or address instead.

02

We ask OpenStreetMap

A live Overpass query pulls every café, coffee shop, restaurant, and fast food spot mapped within a 3 km radius.

03

You get directions

Pick a spot on the map or in the list, and open turn-by-turn directions in one tap — no account required.
